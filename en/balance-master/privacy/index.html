Balance Master Privacy Policy

IdeaBank (the "Developer") respects the privacy of players of the mobile game "Balance Master" (the "Game") and complies with applicable data protection laws. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected while you use the Game, how it is used, and what rights you have over your information.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Collected

The Game has no sign-up or login, and no account is created. We do not collect personal information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address or phone number. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to the Developer's servers.

However, the Game generates a random Support ID on your device when it is first launched, and third-party SDKs built into the Game automatically collect the following information for advertising and analytics purposes.

The Game does not access sensitive device permissions such as contacts, photos, precise location (GPS) or the microphone.

2. Purposes of Collection and Use

  • Serving interstitial and rewarded ads, and capping how often they are shown
  • Measuring ad performance and preventing ad-related fraud
  • Improving the service through statistical analysis of gameplay (such as user retention and per-level clear rates)
  • Identifying the relevant game data when handling an enquiry
  • Verifying in-app purchases and delivering purchased products

4. Consent to Personalised Advertising and Tracking

  • EEA, the United Kingdom and other regions covered by the GDPR: On first launch, a Consent Management Platform (CMP) asks whether you agree to the use of your information for personalised advertising. If you decline, you will be shown non-personalised ads instead, and there is no restriction on playing the Game.
  • iOS: The App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt lets you choose whether to allow tracking across apps and websites. If you do not allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not provided and non-personalised ads are shown.
  • Changing your choice: You can change your consent and tracking preferences at any time — on iOS under [Settings] > [Privacy & Security] > [Tracking], and on Android under [Settings] > [Google] > [Ads].

5. Retention Period

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you run Data Reset from the in-game [Settings] menu or uninstall the Game. Running Data Reset issues a new Support ID. The Developer does not keep a separate copy of this data.

Information collected by third-party SDKs is retained in accordance with the privacy policies of the providers listed in Section 3.

6. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • Withdrawing consent to personalised ads: You can withdraw consent or refuse tracking using the methods described in Section 4.
  • Resetting or deleting your advertising identifier: You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings — on Android under [Settings] > [Google] > [Ads], and on iOS under [Settings] > [Privacy & Security] > [Tracking].
  • Deleting game data: Running Data Reset from the in-game [Settings] menu or uninstalling the Game from your device deletes the stored game data. This data cannot be recovered.
  • Other deletion and access requests: If you wish to have information that has already been collected deleted or disclosed to you, please contact the email address in Section 8. To help us verify your request, please include the Support ID shown in the [Settings] menu. The Developer will act without undue delay after verifying the request; complete deletion within a third-party provider's systems may take up to 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If the Developer becomes aware that such information has been collected, it will be deleted without delay.
