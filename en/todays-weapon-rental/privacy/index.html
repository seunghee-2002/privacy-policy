Today's Weapon Rental Privacy Policy

IdeaBank (the "Developer") values the personal information of users of the mobile game "Today's Weapon Rental" (the "Game") and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected in the course of using the Game, how it is used, and what rights users may exercise with respect to their own information.

1. Items of Personal Information Collected and Methods of Collection

The Game has no sign-up procedure and does not collect personal information that can directly identify a user, such as name, email address, or telephone number. However, in order to provide the Service, the anonymous user identifier (Player ID) generated by Unity Authentication and device information are automatically generated or processed in the course of using the Game as set out below.

ItemMethod of collectionNotes
Anonymous user ID (Player ID)Generated automatically on first launch of the GameA random identifier used for Unity Gaming Services anonymous authentication
Device information (device model, OS version, platform, country and language settings)Collected automatically when the Game is launchedProvision of the Service and statistical analysis
NicknameEntered directly by the user within the GameFor display on the leaderboard
Gameplay data (game progress, ranking scores, etc.)Generated automatically in the course of using the GameCloud save and leaderboard operation
Service usage records (in-game activity events)Collected automatically only where the user has consented in the notice displayed on first launch of the GameStatistical analysis via Unity Analytics

2. Purposes of Collection and Use of Personal Information

  • Providing cloud save, restore, and cross-device transfer of Game Data
  • Operating the leaderboard service
  • Preventing abuse and applying restrictions on use
  • Improving the Service through statistical analysis of game usage (for consenting users only)

3. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information

The information collected is retained until the end of the period of Service use or until the user requests its deletion.

If a user performs a data reset from the [Settings] menu on the main screen, the Game Data stored on the device and the game progress data stored in the cloud are deleted, and a new anonymous user identifier is issued.

Score records registered on the leaderboard, the nickname registered to that account, and analytics data already collected are not deleted by a data reset alone. If you wish to have this information deleted, please make a request to the email address in Section 8 and it will be deleted without delay. Complete deletion within the entrusted processing system (Unity Gaming Services) may take up to 30 days.

4. Entrustment of Personal Information Processing and Overseas Transfer

In order to provide the Service, the Developer entrusts the processing of personal information as set out below, and such information is transferred overseas for processing.

TransfereeUnity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services)

Time and method of transferTransmitted over the network at the time the Game is used

Except for entrustment of processing for the purpose of providing the Service as described above, the Developer does not sell or provide users' personal information to third parties unless otherwise provided by law.

For details on Unity's processing of personal information, see the Unity Privacy Policy.

5. Users' Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • Withdrawal of consent to data collection: You may stop the collection of analytics data at any time from the [Settings] menu on the main screen. No new analytics data will be collected after withdrawal.
  • Data deletion: Performing a data reset from the [Settings] menu on the main screen deletes the Game Data stored on the device and in the cloud.
  • Requesting deletion of other data: For deletion of score records registered on the leaderboard, nicknames, and analytics data already collected, please make a request to the email address in Section 8. Including the Player ID displayed in the [Settings] menu during gameplay will help us verify your identity and process the request smoothly.
  • Other inquiries relating to personal information may be made to the contact in Section 8, and the Developer will take action without delay.

6. Destruction of Personal Information

Where the retention period has elapsed or the purpose of processing has been achieved, the Developer destroys the relevant information without delay. Information in the form of electronic files is deleted by a method that renders it unrecoverable.

7. Personal Information of Children Under the Age of 14

The Game does not collect personal information that can directly identify a user, such as name or contact details, and does not knowingly collect the personal information of children under the age of 14.
